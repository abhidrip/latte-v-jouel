Shipping Policy

At Lattév, every order is carefully packed to ensure your jewellery reaches you safely and beautifully.

Order Processing

* Orders are processed within 2 working days after they are confirmed.
* Orders placed on weekends or public holidays will be processed on the next working day.

Shipping Timeline

* Estimated delivery time is 5–10 business days, depending on your location.
* Delivery timelines may vary slightly for remote areas or due to unforeseen courier delays.

Shipping Charges

* Free shipping on all orders above ₹1,000.
* Shipping charges for orders below ₹1,000 will be calculated and displayed at checkout.

Order Tracking

Once your order has been shipped, you’ll receive a tracking link via email or WhatsApp (if provided), allowing you to track your package until it reaches you.

Delivery Delays

While we aim to deliver every order within the estimated timeframe, delays may occasionally occur due to weather conditions, public holidays, courier issues, or other unforeseen circumstances. We appreciate your patience and understanding.

Damaged, Missing or Incorrect Items

We take great care while packing every order. However, if you receive a damaged package, an incorrect item, or find any item missing, please contact us within 24 hours of delivery.

To process your claim, an uninterrupted unboxing video is mandatory. The video must:

* Begin before the package is opened, clearly showing the sealed parcel.
* Continue without any cuts, pauses, or edits until all contents are removed and shown.
* Clearly display the shipping label and the condition of the products received.

Claims submitted without a continuous unboxing video may not be eligible for replacement or resolution.

Incorrect Shipping Information

Please ensure your shipping address and contact details are accurate before placing your order. Lattév cannot be held responsible for delays or failed deliveries due to incorrect or incomplete information provided by the customer.

Need Help?

If you have any questions regarding your order or shipping, feel free to reach out to us through our Contact page or Instagram DM. We’re always happy to help.





Cancellation, Returns & Exchange Policy

Order Cancellation

* Orders may be cancelled within 12 hours of placing the order.
* To request a cancellation, please send us a direct message (DM) on our official Instagram page with your order details.
* Once an order has been processed or shipped, it cannot be cancelled.

Returns & Exchange

* We do not accept returns or exchanges for any products once they have been delivered.
* As our jewellery is carefully inspected before dispatch, returns or exchanges due to change of mind, personal preference, or incorrect orders placed by the customer will not be accepted.

Damaged or Incorrect Items

In the unlikely event that you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect item, please contact us within 24 hours of delivery.

To be eligible for a replacement or resolution, you must provide:

* An uninterrupted unboxing video starting before the sealed package is opened.
* The video must continue without any cuts or edits until all contents are fully shown.
* Clear photographs of the damaged or incorrect item.

Claims submitted without a continuous unboxing video will not be eligible for replacement or resolution.

Lattév reserves the right to verify all claims before approving a replacement.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What kind of jewellery does Lattév offer?

Lattév offers thoughtfully curated fashion jewellery designed to elevate your everyday style. Our collection includes anti-tarnish pieces that are stylish, lightweight, and made to complement every occasion.

2. Is your jewellery anti-tarnish?

Most of our collections are made using anti-tarnish stainless steel. Product specifications are mentioned on each product page, so please check the description before purchasing.

3. Is the jewellery waterproof?

Our anti-tarnish collections are designed to withstand everyday wear. However, to extend the life and shine of your jewellery, we recommend avoiding prolonged exposure to perfumes, harsh chemicals, chlorine, and salt water.

4. Is your jewellery hypoallergenic?

Most of our anti-tarnish jewellery is skin-friendly and suitable for sensitive skin. If you have specific allergies, please refer to the product description or contact us before placing your order.

5. How long does shipping take?

Orders are processed within 2 working days and are usually delivered within 5–10 business days, depending on your location.

6. Do you offer free shipping?

Yes. We offer free shipping on all orders above ₹1,000.

7. Can I cancel my order?

Yes. Orders can be cancelled within 12 hours of placing them by sending us a DM on our official Instagram page. Orders that have already been processed or shipped cannot be cancelled.

8. Do you accept returns or exchanges?

We do not accept returns or exchanges once an order has been delivered.

If you receive a damaged or incorrect item, please contact us within 24 hours of delivery with an uninterrupted unboxing video. Please refer to our Cancellation, Returns & Exchange Policy for complete details.

9. How can I track my order?

Once your order is shipped, you’ll receive a tracking link via email or WhatsApp (if provided).

10. My package arrived damaged. What should I do?

Please contact us within 24 hours of delivery and share:

* Your order number
* An uninterrupted unboxing video starting before the package is opened
* Clear photographs of the issue

Claims without a continuous unboxing video may not be eligible for replacement.

11. How should I care for my jewellery?

To keep your jewellery looking its best:

* Store it in a dry place.
* Keep it away from perfumes and harsh chemicals.
* Wipe it gently with a soft cloth after use.
* Store it in its pouch or box when not in use.

12. Will the product look exactly like the photos?

We make every effort to display our jewellery as accurately as possible. However, slight colour variations may occur due to lighting and different screen settings.

13. How can I contact Lattév?

You can reach us through our Contact page or send us a DM on Instagram. We’re happy to help with any questions before or after your purchase.
